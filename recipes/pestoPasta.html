Pesto pasta


Description

It's a fresh, good tasting pasta dish, that's from Italian roots, using a pasta of your choice, pesto, and parmeggian cheese.

Ingredients

  • 500g pasta of your choice (I use penne)
  • Parmeggian cheese
  • Basil
  • Salt
  • Pesto

Steps

  1. Take a pot for your pasta, fill it up with water, and put in generous amounts of salt, and bring it to a simmer
  2. Add in your spaghetti without breaking it, and cook it to just over al-dente
  3. Take a cup's worth of pasta water.
  4. Strain your pasta
  5. Mix your pasta, pesto, and a generous amount of parmeggian cheese
  6. Add a bit of your pasta water into the pesto cup, shake it to get everything out and put that into your pasta as well
  7. Server the pasta with a touch of pasil put on top, with some more cheese if you are a cheey person
  8. Enjoy!
